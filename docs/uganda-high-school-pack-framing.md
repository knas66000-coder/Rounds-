# Uganda High-School Pack Framing

Rounds’ high-school category is designed as an **original, offline-first study layer** for Ugandan learners. It is not presented as an official NCDC syllabus, UNEB assessment product, school replacement, or guarantee of examination outcomes. Active subject packs include core sciences, languages, humanities, digital learning, agriculture, ethical studies, and specialist electives.

## Verified framing

The National Curriculum Development Centre’s official e-learning platform presents a dedicated **Secondary section** and identifies its role as supporting curriculum-development orientation and training. Its published secondary materials include a course on **Project-Based Learning for Lower Secondary Competency-Based Education**, supporting a learner-centred approach built around inquiry, evidence, reflection, and practical application rather than recall alone.[1]

The Uganda National Examinations Board publishes upper-secondary curriculum material from NCDC, confirming that NCDC material is relevant across the secondary pathway.[2] Rounds therefore uses “Uganda-focused high school” as a practical framing across lower- and upper-secondary study needs, while avoiding claims that its starter activities are official curriculum coverage.

Each pack should use the same Rounds capabilities already available to university packs: local install state, no-repeat learning rounds, immediate feedback, saved activities, worked calculations or logic traces where appropriate, scenarios, and private multi-step cases. Subject content remains original and specifically labelled as reviewed starter material, not an official NCDC or UNEB resource.

## Coordinated programme scope

The official NCDC eLearning platform maintains a dedicated Secondary section and includes lower-secondary competency-based project learning as well as digital-content support. UNEB also publishes upper-secondary abridged curriculum materials from NCDC and groups Senior 6 materials into broad subject sets.[1] [2] Rounds therefore treats Senior 1–6 as one study journey with a clear lower/upper level distinction, rather than presenting its content as a copy of official subject syllabuses.

The coordinated Rounds completion release provides an active **broad core and specialist learner catalogue**: English, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Geography, History and Civics, ICT and Digital Skills, Agriculture and Food Systems, Religious and Ethical Studies, Kiswahili, Literature, Fine Art, and Technical Drawing. All remain original starter-learning packs, not a representation of official NCDC coverage.

| Programme layer | Rounds implementation standard |
|---|---|
| Senior 1–4 | Foundation-level routing, subject installation, no-repeat learning rounds, local progress, and private reflection. |
| Senior 5–6 | Advanced-level routing with the same subject boundaries and original starter content; no claim of examination prediction or official assessment. |
| Core subjects | Active downloadable packs, reviewed activities, a finite private decision case, saved review, and owner visibility. |
| Specialist electives | Active downloadable Kiswahili, Literature, Fine Art, and Technical Drawing packs with the same reviewed-activity, private-case, local-progress, and owner-visibility safeguards. |
