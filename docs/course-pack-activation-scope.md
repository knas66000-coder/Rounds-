# Rounds All-Program Activation Scope

## Decision

Every current university program in Rounds will now have an **active pack**, not a waiting screen. Active does not mean that Rounds claims a complete, institution-approved degree curriculum. It means that the learner can open a subject-specific local starter experience immediately, with the same privacy, local progress, no-repeat architecture, typed alternative, and clear feedback principles proven in Nursing.

Nursing remains distinct because it has an established clinical question bank and a separate safety boundary. No Nursing question, clinical rationale, or NCLEX label may appear in another program pack.

## Active Starter Coverage

| Program | Active local starter experience | Reusable activity form | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nursing | Existing Nursing Practice | Clinical scenario and oral practice | Existing Nursing safeguards remain unchanged |
| University Foundation Year | Academic Writing and Digital Literacy | Evidence reading and writing planner | Other Foundation Year units remain staged until their activities are added |
| Computing | Clear problem requirements | Evidence reading | Does not claim a full programming syllabus |
| Business | Customer-need evidence | Evidence reading | Does not provide financial or investment advice |
| Engineering | Design constraints | Writing planner | Does not provide safety-critical engineering instruction |
| Natural Sciences | Observation and explanation | Evidence reading | Does not replace laboratory instruction |
| Education | Observable learning objectives | Writing planner | Does not certify teaching competence |
| Social Sciences | Claims and supporting evidence | Evidence reading | Does not diagnose people or groups |

## Pack States

The app will use three visible states. **Installed** means the learner can use an active pack locally now. **Prepare for offline** applies to the Foundation Year collection: its starter assets are bundled in the application and the learner adds the pack to their device library with no network requirement. **In development** applies only to a course unit inside an otherwise active pack when its next reviewed activity has not yet been added.

## Activity Boundaries

The first reusable activity player supports two modes only. An **evidence-reading activity** presents a short original passage, asks one focused question, and provides a bounded explanation. A **writing-planner activity** presents a practical brief, lets the learner write a private draft using structured prompts, and records local completion without claiming to grade the learner’s writing.

All starter items are original Rounds orientation activities. They are deliberately limited, labelled as starter learning, and separate from future institution-mapped course content. The next release can expand each pack only after a qualified reviewer has approved the subject material and the relevant learning outcomes.
