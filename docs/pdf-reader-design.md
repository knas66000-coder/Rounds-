# Rounds Private PDF Reader

The Rounds PDF Reader is an original, **privacy-scoped text reading layer** for learner-owned PDFs. The original PDF remains private and can be opened with a short-lived signed link while connected. The reader extracts readable text into small private sections at upload time, allowing fast topic search, relevant-passage jumps, focused reading, and offline cache after the learner has opened the material once.

The design adapts familiar document-reader patterns without copying interfaces: a compact reader header, a focused reading mode, ranked search results with context, saved passages, a visible progress position, and resume reading. These patterns reflect official document-reader guidance on navigation, bookmarks, text search, highlighted results, and mobile reading modes.[1][2][3]

The reader does not expose a learner's PDFs or extracted text to other learners, Community, Owner Control Center, or generic search. It does not treat uploaded material as clinical authority. For clinical questions, the verified Rounds rationale remains primary; study PDFs provide optional learner-owned context.
