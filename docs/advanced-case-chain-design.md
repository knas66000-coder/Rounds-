# Rounds Advanced Case-Chain Boundaries

## Scope

An advanced case chain may offer a controlled alternative next step after a learner selects an option. Branching exists to make feedback and sequencing more relevant; it does not infer ability, diagnose a learner, assign grades, or create hidden decision paths.

Every chain remains finite, local to the device, and subject-specific. A learner can review the consequences of each chosen option, continue through a defined alternative path, and save a reflection. Reflections and decision histories are private local learning records. They are not synced to the Rounds server, Owner Control Center, other learners, or institutional staff.

## Owner controls

Owner Control stores only case metadata: case identifier, review status, owner note, and update time. An owner may mark a reviewed case as **draft**, **approved**, or **needs revision**. The control center must never retrieve or display learner decisions, reflections, local progress, uploaded documents, voice recordings, passwords, or sessions.

## Release rule

Only an owner-approved case may be described as approved in administrative reporting. The learner experience always states that the case is a learning activity and not an institutional grade, professional instruction, diagnosis, legal opinion, engineering approval, or clinical guidance.
